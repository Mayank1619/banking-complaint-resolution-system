# Resolution Review

## Objective
Implement review logic that checks for completeness, fairness, and policy adherence before a case is closed.

## Business outcome
The bank needs a formal resolution review step to ensure outcomes are fair, complete, and policy-aligned.

## Implemented solution
### 1) Review criteria
- evidence completeness
- consistency with policy and prior decisions
- customer impact assessment
- fairness and clarity of rationale

The review step ensures quality control before a complaint is closed or a refund is approved.

## Acceptance criteria
- The solution is specific, testable, and aligned to the complaint-resolution journey.
- The implementation is evidence-driven and built to be reviewed by both technical and business stakeholders.
- The output includes clear downstream handoff notes for the next stage of work.
