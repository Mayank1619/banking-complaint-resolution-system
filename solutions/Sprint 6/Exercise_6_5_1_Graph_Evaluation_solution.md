# Graph Evaluation

## Objective
Evaluate the multi-agent system against quality, resilience, and traceability criteria.

## Business outcome
The project needs measurable evaluation of quality, reliability, and auditability for the entire graph-based flow.

## Implemented solution
### 1) Quality checks
- resolution accuracy against policy
- time-to-answer and time-to-resolution
- rate of escalations without evidence
- operator satisfaction and explainability

The evaluation framework ensures the graph is judged on trust, resilience, and business value instead of raw model performance alone.

## Acceptance criteria
- The solution is specific, testable, and aligned to the complaint-resolution journey.
- The implementation is evidence-driven and built to be reviewed by both technical and business stakeholders.
- The output includes clear downstream handoff notes for the next stage of work.

## Handoff
This solution is the working artefact for the next sprint or technical phase and should be retained as the canonical implementation narrative for this exercise.
