# P R Review

## Objective
Review a change set critically to ensure code quality, maintainability, and traceability before release.

## Business outcome
The team needs a disciplined review process that enforces quality, context, and maintainability before a change reaches production.

## Implemented solution
### 1) Review checklist
- does the change match the business requirement?
- are validations and status transitions explicit?
- are tests present and meaningful?
- is the change easy to reason about during future maintenance?

### 2) Example feedback
The reviewing team should request clearer status transition documentation and stronger edge-case tests before merging.

## Acceptance criteria
- The solution is specific, testable, and aligned to the complaint-resolution journey.
- The implementation is evidence-driven and built to be reviewed by both technical and business stakeholders.
- The output includes clear downstream handoff notes for the next stage of work.
